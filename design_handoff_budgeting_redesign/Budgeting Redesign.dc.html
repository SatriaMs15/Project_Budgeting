01 — Design direction, revised

A ledger, opened — not a dashboard, skinned

The first pass kept your app's bones — three KPI tiles, two chart cards — and just repainted them in Classical's palette. That's a re-skin, not a redesign. This pass rebuilds the two data-heavy screens as an actual ledger opening: month figures set as bare tabular numerals parted by hairlines instead of boxed tiles, spending-by-category as a numbered contents list with dotted leaders instead of progress bars, and the six-month trend keeps a grouped bar chart (the one place data marks are allowed to fill — bars need to compare at a glance) but thin and square-cornered rather than the rounded-pill bars a default chart library gives you. Everything now also loads the system's real stylesheet and classes (.nav, .btn, .field/.input, .seg, .tag) rather than hand-copied hex values.

What changed structurally:

Tiles → a stat row of bare numerals, no rails, no icon chips.

Grouped bar chart kept, but thin and square-edged, not the rounded-pill default.

Progress-bar category list → a numbered ledger list with dotted leaders, like a table of contents.

Transaction rows → a proper register table (dated columns, outlined category tags) instead of card rows with colored dots.

Type

Cormorant Garamond for headings and figures with presence; Lora for prose and table data. Tabular figures via font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums on both faces — no third, monospace face.
